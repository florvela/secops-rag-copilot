# Insider Threat Response
Respond to suspected insider threat activity: unauthorized data access, bulk downloads, privilege abuse, or off-hours anomalies. Build a full audit trail and escalate to HR and legal.

# Triage
- Enrich the suspected user's context: role, access scope, and recent sensitive-data activity.
- Build a timeline of the user's activity and compare it against their behavioral baseline.

# Containment
- If the risk is high, tighten the user's access and watch for further exfiltration without tipping them off prematurely.
- Preserve evidence (logs, access records) before any account action.

# Notification and closure
- Escalate to HR and legal with the audit trail, and notify the security manager.
- Open an insider-threat ticket and close once the investigation concludes.
